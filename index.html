ANALYST NOTES

How to interpret this view

  • Blast radius: nodes reachable within three relationship hops in this demo graph.
  • Attack path: shortest undirected relationship path, not proof that the route is exploitable.
  • Choke point: a node with high betweenness centrality may connect otherwise separate areas.
  • Alert correlation: sample alerts are linked through shared entities.
Production use would require authenticated telemetry, time-aware edges, access-control semantics, analyst feedback, and security testing.
